What this is, and isn’t

Digital Legacy Vault helps you organise information and share it with the right people. It isn’t a law firm, and it doesn’t make a will legally valid. Please have a lawyer look over your will.

Uploaded documents are stored on our server and protected by your login. They are not locked with a passphrase only you hold, so keep the most sensitive details in encrypted notes instead.

If you choose to notarise a document, only its fingerprint is recorded on a public test network. The file itself never leaves the vault.

What happens when the time comes

Four careful steps, so nothing is ever shared by accident.

  1. Check-ins stop.Your vault is flagged. Nothing is shared yet.
  2. Your trusted contacts are asked.Each one gets a private page asking whether the time has come.
  3. Enough of them agree.You choose how many. Only then does the vault unlock.
  4. Your nominees see their share.Each opens their own link and sees only what you set aside for them.

Questions people ask

Is it free?

Yes. Creating and using a vault costs nothing.

Does this replace a will?

No. It sits alongside your will. It records where things are and what you’d like to happen, so your family isn’t left searching. A lawyer can help you make the will itself legally sound.

What happens if I forget to check in?

Nothing is released. Your vault is flagged and your trusted contacts are asked to review. You can confirm you’re well at any time to reset it.

Who can see what I add?

Only you, by default. Nominees see what you assign to them once the vault is unlocked. Trusted contacts see only a short review page, not your records.

Can I try it before adding anything real?

Yes. The demo vault is filled with sample assets, documents and contacts so you can explore every page.
